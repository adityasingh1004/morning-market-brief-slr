How FY 2026-27 central government dated borrowing adds up: what was budgeted, what has been raised, what is being repaid, and what that leaves as net borrowing. Other government paper, April–July 2026

How the Centre's cash position at RBI moved each fortnight of FY27, and how much of each move the known borrowing flows explain. RBI's weekly balance sheet shows Central Government deposits fixed at about ₹100 crore, because surplus cash is invested rather than held as deposits. So this tab tracks Net RBI Credit to the Centre, published fortnightly (15th and month-end) in the WSS Reserve Money table. When it falls, the Centre's cash surplus with RBI has risen, after removing RBI's own OMO sales, which also cut RBI's holdings of government paper.

RBI operations in FY 2026-27 that changed rupee liquidity or G-Sec supply: outright OMOs, FX swap auctions and the special USD-INR swap facility for FCNR(B), ECB and OFCB inflows.

Dates when tax payments move money from bank deposits into the government's account at RBI. Large payment dates tighten system liquidity for a few days until the government spends the cash back out.

State government securities are auctioned weekly, usually on Tuesdays. RBI publishes quarterly indicative calendars with state-wise, week-wise amounts in annexes, and confirms each week's states and amounts two to three days before the auction.
